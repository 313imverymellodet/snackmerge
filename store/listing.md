# Snack Monster: Google Play listing

**App name** (27/30): Snack Monster: Merge & Feed

**Short description** (67/80): Merge snacks, feed the hungry monster, and don't let it get grumpy!

**Full description** (1190/4000):

```
Drop snacks into the jar. Two of the same snack merge into the next one up the ladder: cherries become strawberries, strawberries become lemons... all the way up to a giant watermelon.

But you are not alone. A hungry monster leans over the jar, craving one snack at a time. Merge the snack it wants and its tongue snatches it right out of the jar for big points and room to breathe. Keep it waiting too long and it gets GRUMPY and stomps the jar!

HOW IT PLAYS
• Aim with your finger, let go to drop
• Match two of a kind to merge them into something bigger
• Feed the monster its craving for bonus points
• Keep snacks below the red line, or it's game over

SPECIAL SNACKS AND POWERS
• HOT PEPPER blows up small snacks nearby
• SPRINKLE CUPCAKE merges with anything it touches
• SHAKE the jar or POP a snack to rescue a messy game

KEEP COMING BACK
• DAILY JAR: the same snacks for everyone each day. Beat your daily best
• Discover all 11 snacks on the ladder
• The monster grows with every meal

MADE FOR PHONES
• Plays offline, no account needed
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected
• Pauses itself when you leave the app
• Sound and vibration can be turned off in the menu
```
